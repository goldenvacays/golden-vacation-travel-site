Good to know
Visa

Jamaican passport holders enter the Dominican Republic visa-free, and the flight from Kingston doesn't pass through the US. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Money

The resort is all-inclusive, so you need very little cash. US$ works everywhere. Carry small bills for tips and the tours. Nobody will take J$.

Weather

Hot and sunny most of the year, same as home. June to November is hurricane season. We watch it and tell you if anything changes.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.

Who replies

On WhatsApp, within working hours, one of our travel professionals is ready to help. Outside those hours our assistant confirms we've got it and someone picks up first thing.
