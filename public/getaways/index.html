Before you go

What you need. Not much.

A Jamaican passport

Valid six months past your return date. We check it before we ticket anything, so there are no surprises at the airport.

Visa-free, no US transit

Panama, Colombia, Peru, the Dominican Republic, Mexico and Brazil are visa-free for Jamaican passport holders. The direct Jamaica to Panama flight is the door to all of Latin America.

Ask on WhatsApp
Can I pay in Jamaican dollars?

Yes. We quote in US$ and show J$ at 160. Pay the deposit and the balance in either, by card link or bank transfer.

Do I need a visa for Panama?

No. Panama is visa-free for Jamaican passports, and Jamaica to Panama City is a direct flight from Kingston or Montego Bay, so you never touch the US. From there you connect to most of Latin America the same way.

How much is the deposit, and when is the rest due?

Usually US$500 on Panama, Medellín and Lima and US$450 on Punta Cana; your quote gives the exact figure. It holds your seat and hotel. The balance is due 30 days before departure. We remind you before we ever release a booking.

What if I have to cancel?

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.

Is baggage included?

Panama and Medellín: one carry-on and one personal item, checked bag US$120 or US$110. Punta Cana: a personal item; carry-on US$70, checked bag US$75. Lima: bags quoted with your dates. Add bags to your quote.
