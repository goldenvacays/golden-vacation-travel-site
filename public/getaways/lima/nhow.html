Questions
Visa

Jamaican passport holders enter Peru visa-free, and the flight from Montego Bay is direct, so there is no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Machu Picchu?

Available on request. Machu Picchu is an experience on its own and needs extra nights added to the trip. Say so when you ask for a quote and we build it in.

Money

Peru uses the sol. Cards work in hotels and restaurants; carry soles for taxis, markets and tips. Change US$ there, not J$. Nobody will take J$.

Weather

Cooler than home. Lima is grey and mild from May to October and warm from December to March. Pack a light jacket either way.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.

Who replies

On WhatsApp, within working hours, one of our travel professionals is ready to help. Outside those hours our assistant confirms we've got it and someone picks up first thing.
