Good to know
Visa

Jamaican passport holders enter Colombia visa-free, and the flight is direct, so there is no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Money

Colombia uses the peso. Cards are fine in hotels and restaurants; carry pesos for taxis, tips and the tours. Change US$ there, not J$. Nobody will take J$.

Weather

Spring all year. Mid-20s by day, cooler at night. Pack a light layer, and an umbrella for afternoon showers.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.
