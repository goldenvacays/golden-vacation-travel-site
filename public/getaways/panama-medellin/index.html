Panama + Medellín

This two-in-one leaves from Kingston: Medellín first, then Panama City on the way home. Visa-free, no US transit, and the hop between them is about an hour. Flights, both hotels and all transfers in one price, per person sharing. J$ at 160.

1
Kingston → Medellín · 4 nightsTequendama Hotel Medellín

You fly out of Kingston and connect in Panama City. Our driver meets you in Medellín and takes you to the hotel. Four nights in the city: Comuna 13, the Metrocable, a day at Guatapé, and the food.

2
Medellín → Panama City · 3 nightsMegapolis · Punta Pacífica

A short hop over to Panama, about an hour. Pick-up at the airport, straight to the hotel. Three nights in the city: Casco Viejo, the Canal, the Cinta Costera, and the mall next door.

3
Panama City → Kingston · directHome

Pick-up from the hotel and a direct flight home to Kingston. Same ticket, no stress.

Good to know
Visas

Jamaican passports enter Panama and Colombia visa-free. The whole trip routes through Panama, with no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Can I do Medellín on its own?

Yes. On its own, Medellín leaves from Montego Bay. This two-in-one is the Kingston option, with Panama City added on the way home.
