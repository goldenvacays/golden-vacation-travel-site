About the hotel

There is a triple room category for three sharing, and two bedroom family rooms and suites. RIU Land kids club and the slides pool make it an easy pick with children. No pets.

Eating and drinking. All-inclusive runs 24 hours. Four restaurants: Balboa, the main buffet; Xian, Asian; Il Palazzo, Italian; and Chiriqui by the pool, lunch and snacks from midday and a steakhouse dinner from 6:30 pm. Five bars: Burbujas in the lobby, the La Rumba lounge bar in the evenings, a 24 hour sports bar, a plaza bar, and the Limon swim-up bar for over 18s.

For children. RIU Land kids club and a children's pool with slides, under the Fun4All family banner.

The practical bits. Free Wi-Fi throughout, a 24 hour gym, free open-air parking, a 24 hour reception with Spanish and English, a discotheque and a souvenir shop. Rooms have air conditioning, a balcony or terrace, a minibar and liquor dispenser, and a safe; some have a sofa bed. Four adapted rooms. Built in 2013 and 2014.

Questions
Visa

Jamaican passport holders enter Panama visa-free, and the flight is direct, so there is no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Money

Panama uses the US dollar. Change your J$ before you leave. Nobody in Panama will take it. Cards work but add fees; cash is easier for taxis and small places.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.

Who replies

On WhatsApp, within working hours, one of our travel professionals is ready to help. Outside those hours our assistant confirms we've got it and someone picks up first thing.
