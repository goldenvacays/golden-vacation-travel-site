About the hotel

Unlimited Luxury means taxes and gratuities are in, there are no wristbands, and dinner at the four a la carte restaurants never needs a reservation. Rooms with two double beds take four people but no more than three adults; every suite sleeps two.

What all-inclusive covers. All meals across eight outlets, top shelf international and domestic spirits, 24 hour room service and concierge, pool and beach wait service, a minibar restocked daily, daytime activities, nightly entertainment and theme parties, the kids club, Wi-Fi, and all taxes and gratuities. Golf, Dreams Spa treatments, tours, the beauty salon, private romantic dinners and the Preferred Club upgrade cost extra.

Eating and drinking. Eight outlets: Bordeaux (French, adults only, formal), Oceana (seafood), Portofino (Italian), Market Cafe for the breakfast buffet, Seaside Grill for the lunch buffet, Barefoot Grill on the ocean, Coco Cafe and the Preferred Club Lounge, with a gluten free menu at every restaurant. Five bars: Rendezvous in the lobby, Manatees swim-up, Barracuda, Desires music lounge until 1am, and the Preferred Club pool bar.

For children. Explorer's Club for ages 3 to 12 runs 9:00 am to 9:00 pm, fully supervised: arts and crafts, a playground, a children's pool, treasure hunts, sandcastle contests, movies on the beach and a children's dinner, with parental beepers. Children must be fully potty trained.

The practical bits. Free Wi-Fi, a 24 hour reception, laundry, a beauty salon and car rental. Small dogs under 10 kg are welcome in set room categories at a nightly charge, with a vaccination certificate at check-in; not at the pool, gym, spa, shops, golf or the beach. Accessible rooms on request.

Questions
Visa

Jamaican passport holders enter Panama visa-free, and the flight is direct, so there is no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Money

Panama uses the US dollar. Change your J$ before you leave. Nobody in Panama will take it. Cards work but add fees; cash is easier for taxis and small places.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.

Who replies

On WhatsApp, within working hours, one of our travel professionals is ready to help. Outside those hours our assistant confirms we've got it and someone picks up first thing.
