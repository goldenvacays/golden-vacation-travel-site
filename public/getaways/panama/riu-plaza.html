About the hotel

Bed and breakfast, not all-inclusive: the buffet runs 6:30 to 10:30 with live cooking stations and gluten-free options, and the city is on your doorstep for everything else. Marbella is walkable to Calle 50's restaurants and a short taxi to Multiplaza.

Eating and drinking. Two restaurants: Arts, the buffet with the breakfast and the live cooking stations, and Tastes, a la carte from midday to 11pm, Monday to Saturday. Three bars, including Sushi Lounge Studio 50 in the lobby from midday to 12:30am and the Ibiza snack bar by the pool from 11am. Room service runs 24 hours, at a charge.

The practical bits. Valet parking at a charge with limited places, free Wi-Fi throughout, a 24 hour reception with Spanish and English, and no pets. Four fully adapted rooms with a walk-in shower, plus eight more with wider doors. Built in 2009 and 2010; the rooms and public areas were refreshed in 2025.

Questions
Visa

Jamaican passport holders enter Panama visa-free, and the flight is direct, so there is no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Money

Panama uses the US dollar. Change your J$ before you leave. Nobody in Panama will take it. Cards work but add fees; cash is easier for taxis and small places.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.

Who replies

On WhatsApp, within working hours, one of our travel professionals is ready to help. Outside those hours our assistant confirms we've got it and someone picks up first thing.
