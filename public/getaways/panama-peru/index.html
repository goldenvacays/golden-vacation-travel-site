Good to know

Visas and passport

Jamaican passports enter Panama and Peru visa-free. Your passport should be valid 6 months past your return.

Money

Panama uses the US dollar. Peru uses soles, and you can change US dollars when you arrive.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary, and we tell you before you pay.
