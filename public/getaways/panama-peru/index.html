Good to know
Visas

Jamaican passports enter Panama visa-free, and Peru visa-free for up to 90 days. The whole trip routes through Panama, with no US transit. Passport valid 6 months past your return to be safe (3 is the minimum). We check it before ticketing.

Machu Picchu?

Available on request. Machu Picchu is an experience on its own and needs extra nights added to the trip. Say so when you ask for a quote and we build it in.

Cancelling

Flights are non-refundable once ticketed. Refunds on the balance vary by hotel. We tell you the rules before you pay.
