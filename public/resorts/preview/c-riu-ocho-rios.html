Questions

RIU Ocho Rios: quick answers

Is RIU Ocho Rios open?

Yes. RIU Ocho Rios is open and taking bookings. Golden Vacation & Travel checked this on 20 November 2026.

Is RIU Ocho Rios good for kids?

Yes. It is the family RIU in Jamaica, with a water park, a slide pool, RiuLand kids club for ages 4 to 12 and a teens club. Swim-up bars are over 18s only.

Which airport is closest to RIU Ocho Rios?

Ian Fleming (OCJ) is about 20 minutes away. Most international flights land at Montego Bay (MBJ), about 1 hour 45 minutes away. A Golden Vacation & Travel private transfer from MBJ is US$125 one way for up to 3 people.

Can Jamaica residents book RIU Ocho Rios in J$?

Yes. Golden Vacation & Travel quotes staycations for Jamaica residents in J$.
