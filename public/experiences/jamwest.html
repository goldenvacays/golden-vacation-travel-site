JamWest Adventure Park

ATV trails through the mangroves, eight zipline towers, horseback into the sea and a half-mile push kart run, twenty minutes outside Negril.

Negril · WestmorelandVisitor rate: booked and paid on the spot

Choose your tour

Two rates, both printed. Visitor rate: Hotel pickup from Negril and Montego Bay included. Paid on the spot, confirmed straight away. Resident rate: Jamaican ID for every guest, shown at the gate. Gate entry only, you make your own way there. Booked by WhatsApp, we confirm and send a card link.

What's included
  • Park entrance
  • Driver and guide throughout
  • Pool and water slide
  • Mineral bath
  • Bird aviary
  • Rock climbing wall
Not included
  • Food and drink (available to buy)
  • Lockers
  • Photos (available to buy)
  • Gratuities
Before you book
  • Bring your Jamaican ID for the resident rate. Without it you pay the visitor rate at the gate.
  • Zipline: 240 lb max, 60 lb min. Horseback from age 9. ATV from age 5.
  • Closed-toe shoes, swimwear and a change of clothes. Most tours get you wet.
  • Not suitable if pregnant or with back or neck problems.
  • Closed 25 December.
Getting there
  • 28 minutes outside Negril, Westmoreland.
  • Visitor rate includes hotel pickup from the Negril and Montego Bay areas. Transfers are per person.
  • Be in the lobby 5 minutes early. The shuttle waits 5 minutes, no longer.
  • Resident rate is gate entry only. You drive.
