Questions about Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica.

Is there hotel pickup from Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica. Mystic Mountain picks up from Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica?

JamWest Braco, about 30 min away. ATV, zipline, horseback and the JamCulture tour, twenty minutes east of Falmouth. Prices start at US$95.

How far is Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica from the airport?

About 45 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 15 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 20 from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$160 one way from Ian Fleming and US$90 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica open?

Not yet. Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica is reopening 1 Dec 2026. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
