Questions about Sandals Caribbean Cay.

Is there hotel pickup from Sandals Caribbean Cay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Sandals Caribbean Cay. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Sandals Caribbean Cay?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 15 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Sandals Caribbean Cay from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Sandals Caribbean Cay open?

Not yet. Sandals Caribbean Cay is reopening 18 Dec 2026. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
