Questions about Sea Garden Hotel.

Is there hotel pickup from Sea Garden Hotel?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Sea Garden Hotel. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Sea Garden Hotel too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Sea Garden Hotel?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Sea Garden Hotel from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Sea Garden Hotel open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
