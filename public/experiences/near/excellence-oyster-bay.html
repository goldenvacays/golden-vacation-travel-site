Questions about Excellence Oyster Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Excellence Oyster Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Excellence Oyster Bay. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Excellence Oyster Bay?

Ocean Coral Spring, about 15 min away. All-inclusive day or evening, families welcome, children half price. It's US$155 per person.

How far is Excellence Oyster Bay from the airport?

About 45 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 15 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$65 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Excellence Oyster Bay open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
