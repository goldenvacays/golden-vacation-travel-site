Questions about Montego Bay cruise port.

What fits a ship day from Montego Bay cruise port?

5 do: Iberostar Selection, Iberostar Waves, JOIA Rose Hall, Ocean Coral Spring and Ocean Eden Bay. We plan to have you back at the pier by 3:30pm, or an hour before the all-aboard time you give us.

Is there pickup from the pier?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's closest to Montego Bay cruise port?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min from the pier. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.
