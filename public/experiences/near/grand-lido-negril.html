Questions about Grand Lido Negril.

Is there hotel pickup from Grand Lido Negril?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Grand Lido Negril. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Grand Lido Negril?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Grand Lido Negril from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.
