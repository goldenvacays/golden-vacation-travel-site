Questions about Dreams Rose Hall.

Is there hotel pickup from Dreams Rose Hall?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Dreams Rose Hall. Mystic Mountain, JamWest Catamaran and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Dreams Rose Hall too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Dreams Rose Hall?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 10 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Dreams Rose Hall from the airport?

About 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Dreams Rose Hall open?

Not yet. Dreams Rose Hall is reopening 16 May 2027. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
