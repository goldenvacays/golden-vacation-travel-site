Questions about Merril's Beach Resort.

Is there hotel pickup from Merril's Beach Resort?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Merril's Beach Resort. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Merril's Beach Resort?

JamWest Catamaran, about 10 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Merril's Beach Resort from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Merril's Beach Resort open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
