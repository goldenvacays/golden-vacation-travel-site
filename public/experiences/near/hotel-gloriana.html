Questions about Hotel Gloriana.

Is there hotel pickup from Hotel Gloriana?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Hotel Gloriana. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Hotel Gloriana too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Hotel Gloriana?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Hotel Gloriana from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Hotel Gloriana open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
