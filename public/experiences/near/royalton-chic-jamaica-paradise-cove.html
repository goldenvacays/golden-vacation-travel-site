Questions about Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove.

Is there hotel pickup from Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove. Mystic Mountain picks up from Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove?

JamWest Braco, about 30 min away. ATV, zipline, horseback and the JamCulture tour, twenty minutes east of Falmouth. Prices start at US$95.

How far is Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove from the airport?

About 45 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 15 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 20 from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$160 one way from Ian Fleming and US$90 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove open?

Not yet. Royalton CHIC Jamaica Paradise Cove is reopening 15 Jul 2027. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
