Questions about Sandals Dunn's River.

Is there hotel pickup from Sandals Dunn's River?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar and JamWest Braco include pickup from Sandals Dunn's River. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$18 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Sandals Dunn's River?

Mystic Mountain, about 15 min away. The Bobsled, the coaster, the zipline, and Dunn's River on the bigger passes. Prices start at US$80. It's closed on Fridays.

How far is Sandals Dunn's River from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Sandals Dunn's River open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
