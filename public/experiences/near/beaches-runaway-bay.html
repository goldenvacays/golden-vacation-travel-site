Questions about Beaches Runaway Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Beaches Runaway Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Beaches Runaway Bay. Mystic Mountain picks up from Beaches Runaway Bay too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Beaches Runaway Bay?

JamWest Braco, about 30 min away. ATV, zipline, horseback and the JamCulture tour, twenty minutes east of Falmouth. Prices start at US$95.

How far is Beaches Runaway Bay from the airport?

About 45 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 15 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 20 from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$160 one way from Ian Fleming and US$90 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Beaches Runaway Bay open?

It's closed for now. Our resort status page shows what's open around it.
