Questions about Hotel 39 Jamaica.

Is there hotel pickup from Hotel 39 Jamaica?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Hotel 39 Jamaica. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Hotel 39 Jamaica too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Hotel 39 Jamaica?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Hotel 39 Jamaica from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay.
