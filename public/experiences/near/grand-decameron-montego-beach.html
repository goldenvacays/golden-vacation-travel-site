Questions about Grand Decameron Montego Beach.

Is there hotel pickup from Grand Decameron Montego Beach?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Grand Decameron Montego Beach. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Grand Decameron Montego Beach too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Grand Decameron Montego Beach?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Grand Decameron Montego Beach from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Grand Decameron Montego Beach open?

It's closed for now. Our resort status page shows what's open around it.
