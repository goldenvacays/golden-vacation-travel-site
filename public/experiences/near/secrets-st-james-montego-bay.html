Questions about Secrets St. James Montego Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Secrets St. James Montego Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Secrets St. James Montego Bay. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Secrets St. James Montego Bay?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 30 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Secrets St. James Montego Bay from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Secrets St. James Montego Bay open?

Not yet. Secrets St. James Montego Bay is reopening 19 Jun 2027. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
