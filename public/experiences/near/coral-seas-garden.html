Questions about Coral Seas Garden.

Is there hotel pickup from Coral Seas Garden?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Coral Seas Garden. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Coral Seas Garden?

JamWest Catamaran, about 10 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Coral Seas Garden from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Coral Seas Garden open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
