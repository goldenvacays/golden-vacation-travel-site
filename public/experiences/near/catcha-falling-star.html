Questions about Catcha Falling Star.

Is there hotel pickup from Catcha Falling Star?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Catcha Falling Star. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Catcha Falling Star?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Catcha Falling Star from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.
