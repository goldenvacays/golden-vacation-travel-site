Questions about Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall.

Is there hotel pickup from Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 10 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall from the airport?

About 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall open?

Not yet. Hyatt Zilara Rose Hall is reopening 1 Jul 2027. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
