Questions about GoldenEye.

Is there hotel pickup from GoldenEye?

Yes. JamWest Braco includes pickup from GoldenEye. Mystic Mountain picks up from GoldenEye too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near GoldenEye?

Mystic Mountain, about 35 min away. The Bobsled, the coaster, the zipline, and Dunn's River on the bigger passes. Prices start at US$80. It's closed on Fridays.

How far is GoldenEye from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.

Is GoldenEye open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
