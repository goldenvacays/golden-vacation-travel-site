Questions about Half Moon.

Is there hotel pickup from Half Moon?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Half Moon. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Half Moon too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Half Moon?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 15 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Half Moon from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Half Moon open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
