Questions about Moon Palace The Grand – Montego Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Moon Palace The Grand – Montego Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Moon Palace The Grand – Montego Bay. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Moon Palace The Grand – Montego Bay?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 10 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Moon Palace The Grand – Montego Bay from the airport?

About 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Moon Palace The Grand – Montego Bay open?

It's closed for now. Our resort status page shows what's open around it.
