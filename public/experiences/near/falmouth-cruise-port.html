Questions about Falmouth cruise port.

What fits a ship day from Falmouth cruise port?

7 do: Ocean Coral Spring, Ocean Eden Bay, Iberostar Selection, Iberostar Waves, JOIA Rose Hall, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko. We plan to have you back at the pier by 3:30pm, or an hour before the all-aboard time you give us.

Is there pickup from the pier?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar includes pickup from Falmouth cruise port. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's closest to Falmouth cruise port?

Ocean Coral Spring, about 15 min from the pier. All-inclusive day or evening, families welcome, children half price. It's US$155 per person.
