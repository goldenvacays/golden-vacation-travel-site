Questions about Round Hill Hotel & Villas.

Is there hotel pickup from Round Hill Hotel & Villas?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Round Hill Hotel & Villas. JamWest Catamaran and Mystic Mountain pick up from Round Hill Hotel & Villas too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Round Hill Hotel & Villas?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 35 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Round Hill Hotel & Villas from the airport?

About 40 min from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$65 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Round Hill Hotel & Villas open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
