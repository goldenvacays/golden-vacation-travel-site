Questions about Rockhouse Hotel.

Is there hotel pickup from Rockhouse Hotel?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Rockhouse Hotel. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Rockhouse Hotel?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Rockhouse Hotel from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Rockhouse Hotel open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
