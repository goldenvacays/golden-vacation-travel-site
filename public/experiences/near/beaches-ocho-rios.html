Questions about Beaches Ocho Rios.

Is there hotel pickup from Beaches Ocho Rios?

Yes. JamWest Braco includes pickup from Beaches Ocho Rios. Mystic Mountain picks up from Beaches Ocho Rios too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Beaches Ocho Rios?

Mystic Mountain, about 30 min away. The Bobsled, the coaster, the zipline, and Dunn's River on the bigger passes. Prices start at US$80. It's closed on Fridays.

How far is Beaches Ocho Rios from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Beaches Ocho Rios open?

It's closed for now. Our resort status page shows what's open around it.
