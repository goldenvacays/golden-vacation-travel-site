Questions about Sunset at the Palms.

Is there hotel pickup from Sunset at the Palms?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Sunset at the Palms. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Sunset at the Palms?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Sunset at the Palms from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Sunset at the Palms open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
