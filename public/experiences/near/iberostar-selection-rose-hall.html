Questions about Iberostar Selection Rose Hall.

Is there hotel pickup from Iberostar Selection Rose Hall?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Iberostar Selection Rose Hall. Mystic Mountain, Poko Loko Floating Bar and JamWest Catamaran pick up from Iberostar Selection Rose Hall too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Iberostar Selection Rose Hall?

Iberostar Waves Rose Hall Beach, about 10 min away. The entry point of the three Rose Hall day passes, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$116.

How far is Iberostar Selection Rose Hall from the airport?

About 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Iberostar Selection Rose Hall open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
