Questions about Charela Inn.

Is there hotel pickup from Charela Inn?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Charela Inn. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Charela Inn?

JamWest Catamaran, about 10 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Charela Inn from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.
