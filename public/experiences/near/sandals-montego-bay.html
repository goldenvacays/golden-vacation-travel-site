Questions about Sandals Montego Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Sandals Montego Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Sandals Montego Bay. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Sandals Montego Bay too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Sandals Montego Bay?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 20 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Sandals Montego Bay from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Sandals Montego Bay open?

Not yet. Sandals Montego Bay is reopening 18 Dec 2026. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
