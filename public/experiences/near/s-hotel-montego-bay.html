Questions about S Hotel Montego Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from S Hotel Montego Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from S Hotel Montego Bay. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from S Hotel Montego Bay too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near S Hotel Montego Bay?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is S Hotel Montego Bay from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay.

Is S Hotel Montego Bay open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
