Questions about Royalton Blue Waters.

Is there hotel pickup from Royalton Blue Waters?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Royalton Blue Waters. Mystic Mountain picks up from Royalton Blue Waters too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Royalton Blue Waters?

Ocean Coral Spring, about 10 min away. All-inclusive day or evening, families welcome, children half price. It's US$155 per person.

How far is Royalton Blue Waters from the airport?

About 45 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 15 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$65 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Royalton Blue Waters open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
