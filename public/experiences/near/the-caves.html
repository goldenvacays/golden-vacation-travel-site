Questions about The Caves.

Is there hotel pickup from The Caves?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from The Caves. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near The Caves?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is The Caves from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is The Caves open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
