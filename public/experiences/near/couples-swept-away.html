Questions about Couples Swept Away.

Is there hotel pickup from Couples Swept Away?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Couples Swept Away. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Couples Swept Away?

JamWest Catamaran, about 10 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Couples Swept Away from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Couples Swept Away open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
