Questions about Hedonism II.

Is there hotel pickup from Hedonism II?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Hedonism II. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Hedonism II?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Hedonism II from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Hedonism II open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
