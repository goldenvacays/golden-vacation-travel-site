Questions about Coyaba Beach Resort.

Is there hotel pickup from Coyaba Beach Resort?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Coyaba Beach Resort. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Coyaba Beach Resort?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 15 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Coyaba Beach Resort from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Coyaba Beach Resort open?

Not yet. Coyaba Beach Resort is reopening, with the date still to be confirmed. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
