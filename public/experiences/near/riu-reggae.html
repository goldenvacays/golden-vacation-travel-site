Questions about RIU Reggae.

Is there hotel pickup from RIU Reggae?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from RIU Reggae. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from RIU Reggae too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near RIU Reggae?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 20 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is RIU Reggae from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is RIU Reggae open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
