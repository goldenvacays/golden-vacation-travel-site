Questions about Ocean Coral Spring.

Is there hotel pickup from Ocean Coral Spring?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Ocean Coral Spring. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Ocean Coral Spring?

Ocean Eden Bay, about 5 min away. Adults-only all-inclusive, day or evening. The evening runs to 2am. It's US$167 per person.

How far is Ocean Coral Spring from the airport?

About 45 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 15 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$65 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Ocean Coral Spring open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
