Questions about Couples Tower Isle.

Is there hotel pickup from Couples Tower Isle?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar and JamWest Braco include pickup from Couples Tower Isle. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$18 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Couples Tower Isle?

Poko Loko Floating Bar, about 20 min away. Jamaica's floating bar. Morning swim, afternoon chill, sunset sips or midnight vibes. It's US$140 per person.

How far is Couples Tower Isle from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Couples Tower Isle open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
