Questions about RIU Palace Tropical Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from RIU Palace Tropical Bay?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from RIU Palace Tropical Bay. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near RIU Palace Tropical Bay?

JamWest Catamaran, about 15 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is RIU Palace Tropical Bay from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.
