Questions about Princess Senses The Mangrove.

Is there hotel pickup from Princess Senses The Mangrove?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran picks up from Princess Senses The Mangrove, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Princess Senses The Mangrove?

JamWest Catamaran, about 30 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Princess Senses The Mangrove from the airport?

About 50 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay and US$265 one way from Ian Fleming.
