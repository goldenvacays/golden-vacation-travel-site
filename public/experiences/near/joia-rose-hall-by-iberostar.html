Questions about JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar.

Is there hotel pickup from JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 10 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar from the airport?

About 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar open?

Not yet. JOIA Rose Hall by Iberostar is reopening 1 Dec 2026. Our resort status page shows what's open around it now.
