Questions about Oasis at Sunset Beach / Sunset Beach Resort.

Is there hotel pickup from Oasis at Sunset Beach / Sunset Beach Resort?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Oasis at Sunset Beach / Sunset Beach Resort. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Oasis at Sunset Beach / Sunset Beach Resort?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 25 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Oasis at Sunset Beach / Sunset Beach Resort from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Oasis at Sunset Beach / Sunset Beach Resort open?
