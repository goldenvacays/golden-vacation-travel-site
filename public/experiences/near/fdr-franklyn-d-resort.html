Questions about FDR, Franklyn D. Resort.

Is there hotel pickup from FDR, Franklyn D. Resort?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from FDR, Franklyn D. Resort. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$18 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near FDR, Franklyn D. Resort?

JamWest Braco, about 30 min away. ATV, zipline, horseback and the JamCulture tour, twenty minutes east of Falmouth. Prices start at US$95.

How far is FDR, Franklyn D. Resort from the airport?

About 45 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 15 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 20 from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$160 one way from Ian Fleming and US$90 one way from Montego Bay.

Is FDR, Franklyn D. Resort open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
