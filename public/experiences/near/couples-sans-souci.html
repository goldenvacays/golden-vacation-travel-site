Questions about Couples Sans Souci.

Is there hotel pickup from Couples Sans Souci?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar and JamWest Braco include pickup from Couples Sans Souci. Mystic Mountain picks up from Couples Sans Souci too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Couples Sans Souci?

Poko Loko Floating Bar, about 15 min away. Jamaica's floating bar. Morning swim, afternoon chill, sunset sips or midnight vibes. It's US$140 per person.

How far is Couples Sans Souci from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Couples Sans Souci open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 64 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
