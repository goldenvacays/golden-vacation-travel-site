Questions about Catalonia Montego Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Catalonia Montego Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Catalonia Montego Bay. JamWest Catamaran, Mystic Mountain and Poko Loko Floating Bar pick up from Catalonia Montego Bay too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Catalonia Montego Bay?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 15 min away. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

How far is Catalonia Montego Bay from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Catalonia Montego Bay open?

It's closed for now. Our resort status page shows what's open around it.
