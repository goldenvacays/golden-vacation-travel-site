Questions about Ocho Rios cruise port.

What fits a ship day from Ocho Rios cruise port?

7 do: Mystic Mountain, Poko Loko, Ocean Coral Spring, Ocean Eden Bay, Iberostar Selection, Iberostar Waves and JOIA Rose Hall. We plan to have you back at the pier by 3:30pm, or an hour before the all-aboard time you give us.

Is there pickup from the pier?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar includes pickup from Ocho Rios cruise port. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's closest to Ocho Rios cruise port?

Mystic Mountain, about 15 min from the pier. The Bobsled, the coaster, the zipline, and Dunn's River on the bigger passes. Prices start at US$80. It's closed on Fridays.
