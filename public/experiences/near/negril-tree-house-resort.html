Questions about Negril Tree House Resort.

Is there hotel pickup from Negril Tree House Resort?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Negril Tree House Resort. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Negril Tree House Resort?

JamWest Catamaran, about 10 min away. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

How far is Negril Tree House Resort from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Negril Tree House Resort open?
