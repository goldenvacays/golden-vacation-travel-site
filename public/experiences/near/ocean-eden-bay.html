Questions about Ocean Eden Bay.

Is there hotel pickup from Ocean Eden Bay?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Ocean Eden Bay. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Ocean Eden Bay?

Ocean Coral Spring, about 5 min away. All-inclusive day or evening, families welcome, children half price. It's US$155 per person.

How far is Ocean Eden Bay from the airport?

About 45 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 15 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$65 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.

Is Ocean Eden Bay open?

Yes, it's open. Every resort on the island is on our resort status page, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
