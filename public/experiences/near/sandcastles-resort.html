Questions about Sandcastles Resort.

Is there hotel pickup from Sandcastles Resort?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar and JamWest Braco include pickup from Sandcastles Resort. Mystic Mountain picks up from Sandcastles Resort too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

What's the closest thing to do near Sandcastles Resort?

Mystic Mountain, about 15 min away. The Bobsled, the coaster, the zipline, and Dunn's River on the bigger passes. Prices start at US$80. It's closed on Fridays.

How far is Sandcastles Resort from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.

Is Sandcastles Resort open?

Yes, it's open. Our resort status page tracks 112 resorts, with reopening dates for the ones that are closed.
