Questions about the South Coast.

What's closest to the South Coast?

JamWest Adventure Park, about 1 h from the middle of the South Coast. ATV, zipline, horseback, safari and push karts. Book the visitor rate on the spot. Prices start at US$65.

Is there hotel pickup on the South Coast?

Not on these. Ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

How far is the South Coast from the airport?

About 2 h from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 30 from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$125 one way from Montego Bay and US$280 one way from Kingston.
