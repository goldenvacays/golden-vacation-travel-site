Questions about Lucea and Green Island.

What's closest to Lucea and Green Island?

JamWest Catamaran, about 40 min from the middle of Lucea and Green Island. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

Is there hotel pickup in Lucea and Green Island?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$5 more per adult. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

How far is Lucea and Green Island from the airport?

About 50 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h 30 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay and US$265 one way from Ian Fleming.
