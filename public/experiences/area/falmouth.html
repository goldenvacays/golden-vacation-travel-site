Questions about Falmouth and Trelawny.

What's closest to Falmouth and Trelawny?

Ocean Coral Spring, about 15 min from the middle of Falmouth and Trelawny. All-inclusive day or evening, families welcome, children half price. It's US$155 per person.

Is there hotel pickup in Falmouth and Trelawny?

Yes. JamWest Braco and Poko Loko Floating Bar include pickup from Falmouth and Trelawny hotels. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

How far is Falmouth and Trelawny from the airport?

About 45 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 15 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$65 one way from Montego Bay and US$165 one way from Ian Fleming.
