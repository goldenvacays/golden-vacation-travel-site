Questions about Montego Bay and Rose Hall.

What's closest to Montego Bay and Rose Hall?

Iberostar Selection Rose Hall Suites, about 20 min from the middle of Montego Bay and Rose Hall. Pools, a children's area, the beach and all-inclusive restaurants, fifteen minutes from Montego Bay. Prices start at US$138.

Is there hotel pickup in Montego Bay and Rose Hall?

Yes. JamWest Braco and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Montego Bay and Rose Hall hotels. JamWest Catamaran picks up for US$30 more per adult. Mystic Mountain picks up for US$25 more per person. Poko Loko Floating Bar picks up for US$10 more per person. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

How far is Montego Bay and Rose Hall from the airport?

15 to 30 min from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 1 h 45 from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$25 one way from Montego Bay and US$155 one way from Ian Fleming.
