Questions about Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay.

What's closest to Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay?

Mystic Mountain, about 15 min from the middle of Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay. The Bobsled, the coaster, the zipline, and Dunn's River on the bigger passes. Prices start at US$80. It's closed on Fridays.

Is there hotel pickup in Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay?

Yes. Poko Loko Floating Bar and JamWest Braco include pickup from Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay hotels. Mystic Mountain picks up from Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay hotels too, at a price shown when you book. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

How far is Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay from the airport?

About 20 min from Ian Fleming, Ocho Rios (OCJ), about 1 h 45 from Montego Bay (MBJ) and about 2 h from Kingston (KIN). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$90 one way from Ian Fleming and US$125 one way from Montego Bay.
