Questions about Negril.

What's closest to Negril?

JamWest Catamaran, about 10 min from the middle of Negril. Sunset sail from Negril, open bar, snorkel stop, Rick's Cafe. It's US$90 per person.

Is there hotel pickup in Negril?

Yes. JamWest Catamaran and JamWest Adventure Park include pickup from Negril hotels. For the others, ask us on WhatsApp if you need a ride.

How far is Negril from the airport?

About 1 h 30 from Montego Bay (MBJ). A private car for up to 3 starts at US$95 one way from Montego Bay.
