How paying works

  • Getaways: a deposit holds your flights and hotel, usually US$500 (US$450 on Punta Cana); your quote gives the exact figure. The balance is due 30 days before you fly, in J$ or US$, by card link or bank transfer. Payment plan available.
  • Tours and airport transfers: you pay by card on the site. Your card is handled by Stripe on its own secure payment page, and we never see or store your card number.
  • Prices on the site are guide prices. We confirm the exact price for your dates before you pay.
