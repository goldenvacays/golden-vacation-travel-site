Copa group fares, answered.

Can we book a Copa group fare online?

No. copa.com takes up to 8 people in one booking, and 10 or more count as a group, handled by Copa's call centre, its sales offices or a travel agency. We handle it for you, with the hotel and transfers.

How many people make a group?

Ten or more, travelling together on the same flights.

Do we need everyone's names now?

No. The seats are held first, and the names are due by the date in your quote.

Where can we fly from?

Kingston (KIN) or Montego Bay (MBJ).

Are you Copa Airlines?

No. We're Golden Vacation & Travel, an independent IATA-accredited travel agency in Jamaica. We book Copa group fares for our clients.
