Privacy policy

Effective 7 September 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you share with us (name, email, phone).
  • Trip details relevant to bookings (dates, destination, preferences).
  • Messages sent via WhatsApp, email, or our forms.
  • Usage data (analytics like page views) to improve the site.

How we use information

  • To provide quotes, make reservations, and deliver customer support.
  • To send confirmations and important trip updates.
  • To improve our services and prevent fraud or abuse.

Sharing

We share necessary details with the hotels, airlines and tour operators that fulfil your booking. We do not sell your data.

WhatsApp

Clicking our WhatsApp buttons opens WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Web). Your messages are governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Card payments on the site

When you book a Golden Experience on this site, your card is handled by Stripe on Stripe's own secure payment page. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Reviews from Tripadvisor

Experience pages show a tour's Tripadvisor rating and its most recent Tripadvisor reviews, including the reviewer's Tripadvisor username. That content is fetched from Tripadvisor each time the page is opened and is not stored by us. It is written by Tripadvisor travellers about the venue and is governed by Tripadvisor's terms.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to safeguard data. We keep records only as long as needed for bookings and legal requirements.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at goldentravellers@outlook.com.
