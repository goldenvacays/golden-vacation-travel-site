Jamaica resorts open after Hurricane Melissa — FAQ

Is Jamaica open after Hurricane Melissa?

Yes — most of Jamaica's all-inclusive resorts are open and welcoming guests. As of 4 September 2026, 36 of the 64 resorts we track are open now and 16 are reopening on set dates through 2026–2027. Jamaica's airports, attractions and the large majority of hotels are operating normally.

What resorts are open in Jamaica right now?

36 Jamaica all-inclusives are open and booking now, across Montego Bay, Negril, Ocho Rios, Runaway Bay and the South Coast — including Sandals, Couples, RIU and Iberostar properties. Which hotels are open in Jamaica after Hurricane Melissa?

Most major all-inclusives are open. A number are temporarily closed for refurbishment and reopening on confirmed dates — the full A–Z list below shows each resort's exact status. We verify and update this list regularly.

How are the resorts in Jamaica after Hurricane Melissa?

Jamaica recovered quickly. The large majority of resorts are open and operating normally; most of the ones that closed did so for planned refurbishments and have confirmed reopening dates. Always confirm your specific resort's status before booking — we keep this page current.

16 resorts are reopening across late 2026 and 2027, in Montego Bay, Ocho Rios, Runaway Bay and the South Coast. Each resort's confirmed reopening date is listed above and in the A–Z below.
