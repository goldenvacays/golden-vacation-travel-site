Jamaica resorts open after Hurricane Melissa: FAQ

Is Jamaica open after Hurricane Melissa?

Yes. Most of Jamaica's all-inclusive resorts are open and welcoming guests. As of 25 November 2026, 87 of the 112 resorts we track are open now and 17 are reopening on set dates through 2026–2027. Jamaica's airports, attractions and the large majority of hotels are operating normally.

What resorts are open in Jamaica right now?

87 Jamaica all-inclusives are open and booking now, across Montego Bay, Negril, Ocho Rios, Runaway Bay and the South Coast, including Sandals, Couples, RIU and Iberostar properties. Search any resort above to confirm, or message Golden Vacation and Travel on WhatsApp for an open resort that fits your dates.

Which hotels are open in Jamaica after Hurricane Melissa?

Most major all-inclusives are open. A number are temporarily closed for refurbishment and reopening on confirmed dates. The full A to Z list below shows each resort's exact status. We verify and update this list regularly.

How are the resorts in Jamaica after Hurricane Melissa?

Jamaica recovered quickly. The large majority of resorts are open and operating normally; most of the ones that closed did so for planned refurbishments and have confirmed reopening dates. Always confirm your specific resort's status before booking. We keep this page current.

Which Jamaica resorts are reopening in 2026?

17 resorts are reopening between 2026 and 2029, in Montego Bay, Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay. Each resort's reopening date is in the reopening table and the A–Z below.

How do I check if a specific Jamaica resort is open?

Search the resort by its current or former name on this page to see whether it's open, reopening (with the date), brand new, or closed. Because many resorts recently changed names, we list the former names too. Still unsure? Message Golden Vacation and Travel on WhatsApp and we'll confirm and find you an open resort.
