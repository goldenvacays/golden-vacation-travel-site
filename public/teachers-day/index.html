Questions

Before you ask.

When is Teachers' Day 2027?

Wednesday 5 May 2027. In Jamaica, Teachers' Day is always the first Wednesday in May.

Which dates do teachers pick?

Most go Friday 21 to Monday 24 May, or Saturday 22 to Tuesday 25 May, over the Labour Day weekend. Those dates go first, so ask early.

What are the cheapest Teachers' Day staycation deals?

Seagarden in Montego Bay, from US$124 a night. Then Bahia Principe Escape and Bahia Principe Explore in Runaway Bay, from US$144 a night. Prices are per person a night, double occupancy, for 21 to 25 May 2027.

Which Teachers' Day deals are closest to Kingston?

The resorts in Ocho Rios and Runaway Bay: Bahia Principe Escape and Bahia Principe Explore from US$144, RIU Ocho Rios from US$180, Moon Palace Jamaica from US$230 and Sandals Ochi from US$316.

Is the price per person?

Yes. Prices are per person a night, double occupancy, for stays between 21 and 25 May 2027. Blackout dates may apply and rates can change, so your quote confirms the price.

Can we stay just two nights?

Yes. Two nights is fine for resort stays in Jamaica.

Can I pay in Jamaican dollars?

Yes. Ask for your quote in J$ and pay in J$.

Our school wants a staff trip. How does it work?

Ten rooms or more gets group rates. Send the dates and numbers through our Groups page and we email the quote within one working day (over 20 rooms, up to five working days). Schools can pay by invoice.

Fewer than ten rooms for the staff?

That books like a regular stay. Use the form on this page or WhatsApp us, and we price the rooms together.

Which resorts are open?

We check what's open before we quote. Our resort status page has the latest.
